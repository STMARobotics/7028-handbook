# Black & White Thinking

Black and white thinking is a cognitive distortion where one views situations in only two categories without acknowledging nuance or gray areas. As much as we love to think that hard and fast rules about design will lead to predictable and consistent outcomes, that is just not the case in robotics, or engineering in general.

Yes, it is important to favor certain characteristics and ideals, but our success thrives in the gray area. Everyone would agree that it would be ideal to check every single box in the *favor* columns, but with limited resources compromises may need to be made.
