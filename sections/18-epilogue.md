# What is 7028 Not Good At?

The design of the team has some downsides. We usually think they're worth the cost, but it's worth noting that there are a number of things we wish we were better at:

- Helping new students find their way. We wrote this handbook to help, but a book can only go so far.
- Disseminating detailed information across platoons.
- Finding and recruiting mentors and students in completely new disciplines (e.g., business and art).
- We miss out on recruiting talented students who prefer to work within a more casual structure. Again, this comes with the territory and isn't something we should change, but it's worth recognizing as a self-imposed limitation.
- Transferring skills and knowledge from veteran members to rookies.

# What is 7028 Not Trying to Be?

7028 is a lot of things, and what it exactly is will change over time as the team grows. These are some things that the team is not and will never strive to be that may not be so obvious:

- A casual, stress free, and simple after school STEM program
- An internship-like program where students are just here to observe mentors
- A student-ran organization

# What Happens When All This Doesn't Work?

Sometimes, the philosophy and methods outlined in this handbook don't match perfectly with how things are going day to day. But we're confident that even when problems persist for a while, 7028 roots them out. As you see it, are there areas of the team in which the ideals in this book are realized more fully than others? What should we do about that? Are those differences a good thing? What would you change? This handbook describes the goals we believe in. If you find yourself in a group or project that you feel isn't meeting these goals, be an agent of change. Help bring the group around. Talk about these goals with the team and/or others.

# Where Will You Take Us?

7028 will be a different team just a few years from now than it is today because you are going to change it for the better. We can't wait to see where you take us. The robots, media, and outreach experiences that you decide to create for the team are the things that will define us into the future. Whether it's a new mechanical design, a control systems innovation, an impact presentation, a robot reveal video, a new manufacturing process, a quality control system, a scouting app, a laser engraved breadstick, a new emoji, or (more likely) something nobody's thought of yet — we can't wait to see what kind of future you choose to build for yourself at 7028.
