# Mission and Values

## Team Mission

*To empower our students to grow into passionate and skilled STEM leaders through competitive robotics.*

## Team Values

- ***Integrity*—** Integrity is more than just telling the truth. Integrity is an all-encompassing idea that you should always do the right thing. This includes having discussions that are honest, making good decisions even when no one is looking, and always seeking the truth.

- ***Self Advocacy*—** Success is yours to own. It's your job to insert yourself wherever you think you should be, and to communicate your needs to coaches and mentors. Be an agent for change.

- ***Growth Mindset*—** Always look for ways to improve not only in skill but in attitude and relationships. Part of having a growth mindset is having humility for your current level of ability or lack thereof. Accept that you have a lifetime of untapped potential and that part of being in 7028 is unlocking that potential.

- ***Excellence*—** Commit to delivering high-quality work that meets or exceeds expectations. Be proud of the work you do by pushing yourself to achieve at a higher level. Don't settle for "just good enough".

- ***Gracious Professionalism*—** Gracious Professionalism is a way of doing things that encourages high quality work, emphasizes the value of others, and respects individuals and the community. Gracious professionals make valued contributions in a manner pleasing to others and to themselves. In the end, Gracious Professionalism is part of pursuing a meaningful life. When professionals use knowledge in a gracious manner and individuals act with integrity and sensitivity, everyone wins and society benefits.

## Team Goals

- **Foster** a welcoming and safe STEM community that challenges our students to become their best self.

- **Grow** our students skills and knowledge through authentic, real-world, mentorship-based experiences.

- **Design and Build** competitive, world-championship worthy robots that we are proud of.

- **Impact** our community through STEM awareness, advocacy, and education.
