# Preface

In 2018, we took the plunge into the world of FIRST Robotics with the expectation
to learn and have fun doing it. Back then, we set out to give our students a valuable
experience tinkering with robots in a simple six week program with little thought
about the impact we would generate in the years to come. Today, 7028 is a world
class robotics team where incredibly talented kids can thrive under challenge, who
may otherwise not have a school community to call home. This handbook is an
encapsulation of our guiding principles that is intended to educate our members
on how we operate. As 7028 continues to grow, we hope that these principles will
serve each new member joining our ranks. If you are new to 7028, welcome!
Although the words in this handbook are important, it's really your ideas, talent,
and energy that will keep 7028 marching into the years ahead. Thank you for being
here. Let's make this an experience you will never forget.
