# Is 7028 Right For Me?

7028 provides a very impactful but also very rigorous experience that does not suit every potential candidate. Oftentimes, the FTC program is a much better option for members who want less commitment, more control over design decisions, and/or less interaction with adults. Sharing any of the following ideas may indicate that FRC is probably not the right program for you, and that FTC may provide you a more valuable experience.

*"I am only able to meet a few times per week, and will rarely stay past 5:00 pm."*

*"I want to participate in a program that is completely student-led, where adults are just supervising."*

*"I am uncomfortable competing with my teammates for my ideas to be adopted on the final product."*

*"I want a more relaxed experience where I can focus on building relationships with my peers rather than progressing the team."*

*"I am just looking for a casual after school activity to explore my interest in STEM."*

*"Collaborating with 40 other students sounds like something I would not enjoy."*

Ultimately, it is up to you to decide what program is right for you. Our goal in offering both programs is to serve as many students as possible in their STEM journey.
