# What We Expect From You

## General Expectations

The only expectation we have for our students and mentors is that they follow our team values and policies. There is no expectation for you to perform to a certain level or commit a certain number of hours to the team.

When you are at an event wearing our logos, always be aware that you are representing our team. Our past and current members have worked very hard for many years to build our reputation to what it is today and it only takes one negative situation to bring that all down. As with anything, we expect you to follow our team values and policies while at events as well.

## Failure and Risk

At 7028, you will never be reprimanded for making an honest mistake. It wouldn't make sense for us to operate that way. Providing the freedom to fail is an important trait of the team — we couldn't expect our students to grow if we also penalized them for errors. We can always repair the mistake or make up for it.

Screwing up is a great way to find out that your assumptions were wrong or that your model of the world was a little bit off. As long as you update your model and move forward with a better picture, you're doing it right. Look for ways to test your beliefs. Never be afraid to run an experiment or to collect more data.

It helps to make predictions and anticipate nasty outcomes. Ask yourself "what would I expect to see if I'm right?" Ask yourself "what would I expect to see if I'm wrong?" Then ask yourself "what do I see?" If something totally unexpected happens, try to figure out why. There are still some bad ways to fail. Repeating the same mistake over and over is one. Not listening to teammates or mentors before or after a failure is another. Never ignore the evidence; particularly when it says you're wrong.
